Get your emails in front of the right college coaches.

NextLevel helps baseball players email college coaches. You tell us about yourself, and we write a different email for each coach.

How it works

1

Tell us about you

Your position, grad year, stats, GPA, and a link to your highlight video.

2

Get matched

You get a short list of coaches at your level who are close to home.

3

Send your emails

Each email talks about that coach's program and your own numbers.

4

Follow up

See who opened and who replied. After ten days we remind you to follow up.

I played junior college baseball at Trinidad State, then at two four year schools. I sent a lot of emails to coaches and most of them never answered. I made NextLevel so you do not have to guess what a coach wants to read.

Erick, founder and former college baseball player

Use it like an app

NextLevel is a progressive web app. It is a website, but you can also install it. On your phone, open your browser menu and tap Add to Home Screen. It gets its own icon, opens full screen, and still loads with no internet.
